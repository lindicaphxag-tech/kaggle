# Default EEG — Positive Mechanisms First (2026-10-15)

**Research-policy change, not an accomplished result or selective reporting authorization.**
All new promotion decisions prioritise a *constructive algorithm, independently measurable benefit and falsifiable scope*. Negative or null experiments remain in full logs, SI and limitations, with original outcomes unchanged.

## P1 / JNE — risk-aware source-only regularisation
- Current support: one previously measured EEGMMIDB frozen n=20 comparison reports a positive +0.0561 AUC against a **historical** covariance-score comparator, Holm p=.00597; five people were harmed. Other newer comparators / held cohorts are mixed or negligible. **Not a universal algorithm improvement.**
- Constructive target: predict independently from source recordings when covariance regularisation should differ from discriminative regularisation; abstain or default to fixed strong policy otherwise.
- Required trial: frozen train/validation rules, previously unseen acquisitions, robust competitive fixed policies, held-person paired AUC, harmed-person fraction and calibration of predicted action gain.
- Promotion: positive median acquisition effect and no unacceptable harm frequency versus well-tuned comparator, with confidence bands; do not suppress harmed cases.

## P2 / BSPC — reusable signal-content provenance and safe evaluation
- Already positive, VERIFIED DIGITAL PRODUCT evidence: 50 released IDs, 49 exact processed-content groups; all 1,225 released-ID pairs audited at 29 named EEG channels x 320 native records. Actual same-content 4/5, exact aligned partial blocks 47/49 and 11/15. Five publicly released acquisition datasets checked for exact processed epoch overlap.
- Current evaluation is a retrospective correction, NOT a decoder gain: nominal 50-ID macro 0.594000 versus 49-content reporting 0.588673 and full refit 0.582551. Most matched change concentrates in one alias-affected group.
- Constructive method target: `ProvGuard-EEG` **working name only** (do not claim novel algorithm yet): fingerprint channels and native time blocks; derive *multiscale* constraints preserving exact checks while permitting missing channels, permutations, shifts and separately declared preprocessing transformations; issue an auditable abstain/investigate decision; construct content-group-disjoint splits.
- Strong baselines: whole-file hash, canonical trial hash, per-channel exact hash, natively aligned fixed-block hash, Pearson/correlation near-match, waveform-aware approximate matching and standard split hygiene.
- Independent tests: inject known controlled re-export transformations into isolated training corpora; freeze positive/negative test definitions; evaluate actual sensitivity at a bounded false-alarm rate, partial-segment localisation error, runtime, abstention and split violation rate. Evaluate on at least one previously unused released EEG dataset separately, without equating identity of signal samples to patient identity. DO NOT assume numeric gains or claim superior FPR until measured.
- Main P2 BSPC author-review manuscript stays evidence-first and scoped; a new method should supersede its claims ONLY if benchmarked and distinct in contribution. Do not invent MAT-to-EDF ancestry; follow up with data stewards.

## P3 / PLOS ONE — source-only selective transfer policy
- Current experiments do not demonstrate stable improvements over informed fixed-source baselines; existing negative-transfer observations are not a novel winning algorithm.
- Constructive target: source-only predictive trust/abstention with calibrated risk bound, measured *reliable action value* in target-blind held acquisitions.
- Promotion: independent held-acquisition improvement over best fixed policy and source CV selection, report coverage, accuracy, harm rate; split strictly before fitting decisions.
- If no independent positive mechanism, HOLD the standalone P3 manuscript. Do not reframe negative AUC as benefit.

## P4 / Numerical Algorithms — genuinely superior certified ranking procedure
- Current historical coverage increase ~0.146 percentage points on one exposed Weibo source fold is conditional and small; not evidence of new numerical superiority.
- Constructive target: prove outward-rounded end-to-end input-to-rank bounds, with certified whole-shrinkage intervals and a hybrid rational/exact isolation algorithm that is provably tighter or faster than established strong methods.
- Promotion: independent numerical families, challenging conditioning, runtime-coverage-Pareto, correctness against exact rational oracle, and an explicit theorem whose assumptions match floating-point execution.
- If only a conditional exact-rational post-feature exercise, keep as theory/supplement; do not promote to novel Numerical Algorithms full paper.

## Scientific reporting and publication gates
1. Freeze hypotheses and baselines before seeing new held results; prevent post-hoc metric/subject cherry picking.
2. Mechanism originality must be distinguished from rediscovered dataset error, known hashing, standard component grouping and known interval certification.
3. Publish null and harmed-person results accurately in relevant main text or SI, even when an overall metric is positive.
4. Headline a method as superior only after a positive effect on genuinely unseen data with appropriate uncertainty and strong baselines.
5. Existing P2 authorship, affiliations, ethics, funding, conflicts, AI disclosure, data-custodian clarification and formal BSPC submission still require human approval; no submission has been executed.
