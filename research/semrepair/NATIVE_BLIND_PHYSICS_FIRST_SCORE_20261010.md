### Crucial strong-baseline correction

The model-based **independent** uniform-prior optimal active diagnosis oracle (exact Fraction Bellman over measured signature partitions) reports an expected cost **4** in each of the three forcing regimes. The measured fixed staged policy's realized mean probe cost is likewise **4**. There is **NO algorithmic expected-cost advantage over the optimal finite active-diagnosis baseline**. This parity is checked in live CI, not inferred from paper prose.

**What this positively establishes:** internal taps can resolve ambiguities that are unidentifiable from external trajectories, and access/risk constraints meaningfully affect whether a concrete repair can be authorized; real simulator replay confirms the declared repairs close the expected model effects.

**What it does NOT establish:** general system identification; novel optimal decision trees; a speedup over the exact active-diagnosis oracle; a new robot system/controller family; out-of-family nuisance calibration; independent pretrained robot policy task recovery; or physical hardware safety.

## Next original research discriminator — required before T-RO/RA-L flagship claim

1. Recruit or independently generate a *different actuator/controller family* and implementation-bound repair objects without revealing ground truth to the method.
2. Freeze unseen source worlds, physically executed tap risk/cost, exact code/model/hardware (if used) hashes and uncertainty model before execution.
3. Run a matched best **repair-class-aware decision-tree / information-gain / generic active identification** baseline with exactly the same allowed readouts and risk.
4. Compare safe authorization, refusal, wrong repair, end-effector physical effect, task success, and per-episode probe cost **separately**; never treat a zero-observation error in 12 correlated test cells as a statistical safety guarantee.
5. A method that merely matches the optimal diagnosis oracle on known world signatures should be presented as a useful *application/representation*, not claimed as a novel superior stopping algorithm.

### Publication decision

**Mechanism verified on native finite physics; novel general algorithm and independent transfer NOT established.** Keep the paper a draft. The genuinely decisive next experiment is an out-of-world-family repair decision with latent contracts that a generic optimal identification baseline cannot exploit under an equal observation/cost model.
