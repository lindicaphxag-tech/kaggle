# ClinGeom | Nature Methods / TMI / MedIA precedent and evidence boundary

**No positive segmentation-efficacy claim** for the current hand-gated output-fusion method. Two frozen authentic public MRI runs (six source patients each) and independent download/hash/score audit in https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/37983549282 yield *12 distinct public-source patients*, 3 fixed 64-cubed ROIs each, **0/12 gated Dice improvements vs canonical-first**, patient-mean Dice 0.151174826 native vs 0.151102822 gated, delta -0.0000720034. The 36 correlated ROIs cannot be treated as 36 patients, and this is NOT a multicenter or whole-brain result.

**Potential distinct conceptual contribution:** typed source-to-acquisition coordinate transformations with a proof that a feature cannot be merged before spatial provenance validity is established, plus a measurable new repair that preserves strong unpermuted predictions. Prior implementations that reorder only final outputs are basic spatial correctness, not automatically new ML.

**Required axes:** source-vs-target patient physical affines, orientation/permutation plus voxel spacing, real pretrained feature tensor at a frozen cut layer, exact before/after decoder output parity and fair canonical-first comparator, task-level Dice and physical HD95 with robust empty-surface convention, entire patient volumes/site separation, per-patient paired intervals. Include **failure cases and both negative cohorts**.

**Editorial decision:** the current method is a technically honest negative pilot; NO-GO for a TMI or MedIA 'new superior segmentation method' paper. New method requires new independent untouched patient/domain data, nontrivial novelty over ubiquitous orientation/resampling primitives, and actual large effect in medically relevant units. No invented claims or repeated slicing of exposed cohorts.
