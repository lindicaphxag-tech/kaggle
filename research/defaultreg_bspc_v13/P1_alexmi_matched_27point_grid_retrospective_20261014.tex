\paragraph{Protocol-matched 27-point source-only AlexMI sensitivity.}
The AlexMI study in Table~\ref{tab:p1-alexmi}
used a shorter eight-point shrinkage grid than
the main experiment. We therefore replayed the
\emph{same already exposed} source IDs 1--6 and
held IDs 7--8 with the original 27-point grid
\(\{0,.0005,.001,.002,.003,.005,.0075,.01,.015,
.02,.03,.05,.075,.10,.15,.20,.30,.40,.50,
.60,.70,.80,.90,.95,.98,.99,1\}\), without
changing features, the independent LDA implementation,
the fitted-mean predictive Gaussian score or source
participant folds. The source-only criteria selected
\(\lambda_{\rm macro}=1\) and
\(\lambda_{\rm proper\ NLL}=.20\),
with a source-fold forecast advantage of
\( +.05417\) AUC. The two \emph{previously
inspected} held people yielded:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrr}
 \mathrm{ID}&\mathrm{macro\ AUC}&
 \mathrm{proper\ NLL\ AUC}&\Delta \mathrm{AUC}\\\hline
 7&1.0000&.9950&+.0050\\
 8&.9950&.8075&+.1875
\end{array}
\]
Their descriptive mean margin is \(+.09625\) AUC,
versus \(+.07750\) under the eight-point grid.
The originally frozen source decision was committed
\emph{before the re-read within this workflow},
but the outcomes of both targets had been examined
by the project in the eight-grid study, so this
is a \emph{retrospective} common-grid sensitivity
check, not new prospective or cross-acquisition
evidence. The original and full-grid executable
source and scalar outcome receipt are archived in
\href{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38536408103}
{GitHub Actions run 38536408103}.
Macro predicted the positive advantage for both;
a trivial always-positive forecast predicted it too.
Neither predictive gain over that null nor
a novel held-person decision policy is established.
