\subsection{Additional independent-acquisition estimand comparison on BNCI2014\_001}
\label{sec:p1-bnci001-external-estimand}
We added a distinct public motor-imagery acquisition,
BNCI2014\_001, using released participants 1--9,
left/right imagery, C3/Cz/C4, 8--30~Hz, 160~Hz and
a fixed 27-point scalar shrinkage grid.
Six prespecified source-only policies were compared:
grouped macro-AUC, proper source-trained-class-mean
Gaussian predictive NLL, historical validation-class-centred
CovScore, Ledoit--Wolf, OAS, and a fixed $\lambda=0.1$.
This is a new computation on an existing public
acquisition, \emph{not} a preregistered independent
prospective recruitment or clinical validation.

In one traceable chronological stage, the source
parameters for eight released persons were selected
and serialized as SHA-256
\texttt{a0fa2206f9ec} (complete SHA-256 in the archived source manifest)
\emph{before the ninth person's EEG was read by that script}.
For the held ninth participant (288 trials), source-macro
scored 0.746480 ROC-AUC and the corrected predictive-NLL
source policy 0.709394; the difference was
$+0.037085$ AUC for source-macro. These choices
were source-only within the run but were formulated
after related EEG studies; the chronological file lock
is not independent preregistration.

The corresponding nine-person leave-one-person-out
reanalysis, completed after opening the ninth participant,
is explicitly retrospective. Each held fold refitted
the same tangent reference and predictive/discriminative
source models on eight other participants; source
training sets overlap heavily.

The mean source-macro minus proper-predictive
difference was $+0.009265$ AUC, with two
participants differing by more than 0.02 AUC
in favour of source-macro and no participants
differing by more than 0.02 in the other direction.
This differs in magnitude from earlier corrected
predictive controls (including Shin2017A), again
suggesting acquisition-dependent source-selection
behaviour rather than a universal performance law.
The executed source-lock, individual outcomes and
frozen experiment artifact are available at
\url{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38326645328}.
The result validates the need to name the
proper predictive estimand, but does not
establish a superior decoder or general prospective
selection policy.
