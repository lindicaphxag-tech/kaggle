\subsection{Endpoint-specific multichannel reuse and content reuse}
Two logically distinct endpoints are required. The coherent recording
endpoint, $E_{\mathrm{clock}}$, requires at least four distinct
channel pairs to admit a single global time lag. The content
endpoint, $E_{\mathrm{any}}$, accepts verified reuse of at least one
individual channel even when different channels have incompatible
time offsets. Accordingly, an asynchronous multi-sensor composite
is a designed negative for $E_{\mathrm{clock}}$ but a positive for
$E_{\mathrm{any}}$. A split claimed to avoid \emph{identified numerical
reuse} must group connected records using confirmed
$E_{\mathrm{any}}$ edges, not solely the coherent-recording edges.

The same frozen CHB--MIT digital EDF controlled-transformation
experiment was evaluated under both pre-specified endpoint labels.
For six original public EDF recordings, $E_{\mathrm{clock}}$
detected 48/48 transformed positive cases with 0/30 constructed
negative matches; $E_{\mathrm{any}}$ detected 60/60 constructed
positive cases with 0/18 constructed negatives. The additional
12 $E_{\mathrm{any}}$ positives are six single-channel reuse and
six asynchronous same-source reuse cases. Every accepted numerical
correspondence was independently verified under the trusted
algorithm policy. Complete case-level results and source hashes
are emitted by the GitHub Action run
\url{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38671905017}
(status SUCCESS, inspected 15 October 2026).

These are \emph{controlled modifications of six independent
public EEG digital source records}, not 78 statistically independent
recordings and not clinical duplicate ground truth. Under the
narrow $E_{\mathrm{clock}}$ endpoint, the pre-existing
shared-clock algorithm and the updated verified matcher
were tied at 48/48 positive matches and 0/30 negatives;
the unconstrained independent-lag Hungarian comparator
matched 48/48 positives but falsely accepted six
asynchronous composites. No published DTW synchronizer
superiority or population false-positive guarantee follows.

\subsection{Numerical witness scope and prospective research extensions}
For an accepted numerical witness, an external checker recomputes
the per-channel lagged training and blocked verification
correlations, validates the one-to-one channel correspondence,
the common lag, all reported overlapping sample intervals,
and standardized floating-array digests. Verification certifies
the stated numerical predicates on the supplied arrays. It does
\emph{not} prove physical acquisition origin, patient identity,
global optimality or a probabilistic false-match rate.

A subsequent V5 research prototype explores continuous
whole-search scores evaluated on an untouched temporal block,
record-level null calibration, and matched-channel common-mode
residual evidence against shared nuisance artifacts.
Neither an improvement in natural EEG provenance recovery nor
a strong published-baseline advantage has yet been established
for V5. These extensions are therefore \emph{not included} in
the V4 empirical result claims and must pass separate
frozen independent-source experiments before promotion to the
primary method.
