\paragraph{Independent-run source-feature precision forensics.}
\label{sec:p3-dreyerb-float-fingerprints}
The complete 256-bit hashes are stored in the archived machine-readable receipts. The earlier Dreyer2023B source covariances
failed a strict bytewise replay from their
original source SHA-256,
\texttt{7146797e\ldots}, into
\texttt{9f650460\ldots}.
To distinguish a systematic changed source
pipeline from trial reordering or within-run
nondeterminism, we obtained two independently
executed, \emph{source-only} GitHub Actions
fingerprint archives. Each of their original
runs requested only IDs 61--68 twice and
never requested IDs 69--76; the repeated
source reads were \emph{exactly byte-equal
within each runner}.
In contrast, between the runners, the
per-participant raw float64 covariance SHA
and the order-insensitive labelled-epoch
digest differ for all eight persons, while
the exact \emph{quantised} fingerprints
agree for all eight: normalised covariance
shape rounded at $10^{-9}$ precision and
per-epoch $\log_{10}$ covariance trace
rounded at $10^{-8}$ precision both give
eight matching per-person hashes.
The maximum difference in per-person
\emph{mean} covariance trace is
$1.43\times10^{-14}$; the maximum difference
in mean log-determinant is
$1.78\times10^{-15}$.
These are measurements of \emph{summary
statistics}, not bounds on the largest
elementwise covariance or score difference.
The executable, two-original-receipt comparison
is pinned to original source-only runs
\texttt{38548578442} and \texttt{38548578441}.
The independent two-artifact replication completed
successfully in
\href{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38552831272}
{GitHub Actions run 38552831272},
which checked the original input SHA-256 values,
all 8 per-person exact and quantised fingerprints,
and the non-disclosure of held target EEG.

Taken together, these observations are
consistent with floating-point roundoff
rather than gross source-record replacement;
they do \emph{not} establish identical raw
acquisition file bytes, identical fitted
model parameters, or an IEEE-754 perturbation
guarantee. Quantisation can hide a true
difference below its resolution and does
not make an exact lock pass. Accordingly,
the six-person fixed-choice source-\emph{refit}
reported in Table~\ref{tab:p3-dreyerb-six}
remains a lower-tier sensitivity test, not
a resurrection of the failed exact-source
replay. A future strict replay must persist
and reuse the exact source feature arrays
and model weights rather than reproducing
floating-point hashes from new recomputation
and assuming equality.
