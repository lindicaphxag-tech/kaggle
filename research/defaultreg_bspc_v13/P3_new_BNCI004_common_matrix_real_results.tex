\subsection{New shared-candidate experiment: BNCI2014\_004}
\label{sec:p3-bnci004-first-run}
To resolve the principal design weakness of our
heterogeneous negative-case series, we ran a new
\emph{same-candidate-matrix} experiment on the public
BNCI2014\_004 two-class left/right motor-imagery
dataset (nine released people; three scalp channels).
The source-only policies were precisely the same
source-macro 27-point LDA selector, proper
source-class-mean Gaussian predictive-NLL selector,
historical validation-class-centred covariance score
(which must not be called predictive likelihood),
Ledoit--Wolf and OAS shrinkage, and a non-tuned
$\lambda=0.1$ control. All selectors used 8--30~Hz,
160~Hz and train-group-fitted tangent geometry.
Candidate parameters were selected from
participant-grouped source folds without the
corresponding held participant's EEG or outcome.
The proper and class-centred criteria are
known estimands, not methods newly invented
in this companion research note.

In a separately preserved chronological stage, only
released IDs 1--8 were processed initially.
The eight-source candidate choices and processing
receipt were saved with SHA-256
\texttt{2eeeaa143c61} (full SHA-256 in the published workflow artifact)
\emph{before opening participant 9's EEG}.
On 720 held participant-9 labelled trials,
source-macro yielded $0.836798$ ROC-AUC,
proper predictive NLL $0.837454$,
historical class-centred score $0.836111$,
Ledoit--Wolf $0.836150$, OAS
$0.836165$ and a fixed $\lambda=0.1$
$0.837160$. Thus the proper-NLL selector
was $+0.000656$ AUC above source-macro
on \emph{one} held person, which
is far too little information to claim
a typical benefit or non-benefit.

After the reserved first-run result was opened,
we evaluated all nine participant-held folds
using each of the same six selectors and eight
other source participants per fold. We explicitly
label these nine cross-validation folds as
\emph{exploratory}: they overlap in training sources,
include already exposed recording data and
are neither independent new acquisitions nor
separately pre-registered replications.

This unified matrix is scientifically more
interpretable than comparing different methods
on disjoint exposed datasets. It does not
establish any population-level ordering,
since the number of participants is small,
their source training sets are shared and
the original 8--30~Hz montage is intentionally
low dimensional ($p=6$ tangent features).
It neither confirms the earlier four different
proposals' null hypotheses nor qualifies as
two new independent external datasets.
Executable source lock, individual held-group
outcomes and fully auditable code are attached
to successful GitHub Actions
\url{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38321155451}.
