\subsection{New named external BNCI2014\_002 correct-likelihood cohort: a near-null stress test}
\label{sec:p1-bnci002-corrected-subset}
We conducted a separate, internally source-locked test
on the previously \emph{unused under this exact project
protocol} public BNCI2014\_002 motor-imagery acquisition
(right-hand versus feet imagery), independently
documented as 14 public participants with
15 native Laplacian EEG channels. Ten source
participants (released IDs 1--10) were loaded first;
the original paper's entire 27-point shrinkage grid
was used to select source-macro AUC and
source-training-mean \emph{proper predictive}
Gaussian log-score parameters on grouped source folds.
The two parameters, fixed $\lambda=0.1$ LDA,
and tangent-logistic $C=1$ baselines were
committed to an SHA-256 source-only receipt
\texttt{32001bac\ldots c3d2fea2ee}
\emph{before} the first read of any held
participant 11--14. The experiment selected
the central \emph{array positions} 7, 8 and 9
(out of all 15 native EEG channels, with
zero-based positions 6--8); these positional
inputs must \emph{not} be described as
C3, Cz and C4, or assumed equivalent
to the older all-15-channel analysis.
This deliberately different 3-channel
representation makes it a
representation/protocol \emph{sensitivity},
not a direct new replication of the
earlier all-15-channel CovScore family.
The first source-only read failed
before accessing \emph{any} target because
a predeclared physical-name assumption
(\texttt{EEG7} etc.) did not match the
MOABB loader's actual native labels;
the channel-position correction was
made before the successful source lock
and held outcomes. The completed
source/target trace, including the failed
first run, remains available in GitHub.
The independent public dataset is not
an independently \emph{registered} new
trial and four held people in one study
are not four independent acquisitions.

The four individual paired
Macro-minus-proper differences were
$+.0109375$, $+.0003125$,
$+.00578125$ and $-.01109375$ AUC:
a descriptive average of only
$+.001484375$.
Macro selection was actually below
both the fixed-$\lambda$ LDA and
fixed tangent logistic \emph{in mean}
on this group of four newly read
participants, although with different
per-person patterns. This new
non-confirmatory study-level result
weakens any claim that the AlexMI
macro--proper difference predicts
a large generic out-of-source EEG
advantage, and strengthens the
necessity of condition-specific
and simple-baseline comparisons.
The complete source-lock and real
result are from the successful
\href{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38557289971}
{GitHub Actions run 38557289971}.
No meaningful predictor or new
personalised selection gain over a
trivial always-macro action has
been established by this contrast.
