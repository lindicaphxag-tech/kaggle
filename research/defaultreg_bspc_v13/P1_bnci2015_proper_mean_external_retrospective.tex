\subsection{BNCI2015\_001: corrected predictive NLL}
\label{sec:p1-proper-bnci2015-new}
To determine whether the corrected comparator eliminates
the historic covariance-score contrast in a second
previously exposed dataset, we separately recomputed
leave-one-participant-out source-only shrinkage choices
on the public 12-person BNCI2015\_001 motor-imagery
acquisition (right hand versus feet; 13 channels).
The 27-point scalar shrinkage grid, 8--30~Hz processing
and 160~Hz resampling were pinned for every held person.
Each outer training pool contained the remaining 11
participants. Five participant-grouped \emph{inner
source} folds each refitted the tangent reference,
class means and pooled within-class covariance.
The corrected Gaussian predictive-NLL criterion
scored source-validation EEG using \emph{source-training}
class means, whereas the historical comparator used
each held-source-validation participant's own
class means. The source-macro criterion selected
on inner grouped ROC-AUC; Ledoit--Wolf and OAS
were computed as separate analytic controls.
All 12 source parameter choices were serialized and
SHA-256 locked before computing the \emph{new}
per-participant summary, but the target dataset had
already been evaluated by earlier related studies:
this is an explicitly \emph{retrospective}, not an
independent prospective, comparison.

Source-macro exceeded proper predictive NLL
on average by $0.035989$ AUC, but three of
the 12 participants were materially harmed
($\Delta<-0.02$ AUC) and five improved by
more than $0.02$ AUC. The exploratory
two-sided exact paired sign-flip analysis,
enumerating $2^{12}=4096$ sign assignments
on the participant-level paired differences,
yielded $p=0.157227$.
This \emph{does not} reject equality
at the conventional 0.05 level and is
not added retrospectively to the original
Holm-adjusted confirmatory comparison family.
The direction does not contradict earlier
proper-NLL contrasts: Shin2017A was
$-0.000307$ and a separate BNCI2014\_002
comparison $+0.006819$; taken together
the previously exposed acquisitions show
effect heterogeneity, not an empirically
validated universal transfer rule.

The public-data, pinned-code and per-person
numerical receipt are available in the
successful real EEG workflow
\url{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38321486917}.
No target labels or target-specific covariance
are involved in choosing a participant's
source training policy, but all these
observations are retrospectively inspected.

\paragraph{Source-to-held directional prediction check.}
For each of the 12 outer holds, we additionally recorded the
source-only inner-fold AUC difference between the selected
source-macro and proper predictive-NLL shrinkage choices
before evaluating that held participant. The first diagnostic reused the selected inner-source
folds and predicted the held-participant AUC direction
in 8/12 cases, with mean absolute error 0.070620 AUC.
In a subsequent source-choice cross-fit, each source-validation
group was excluded when choosing both policies for that group.
The resulting source contrast predicted the outer held-person
direction in only 7/12 cases, with mean absolute error
0.071948 AUC. This falsifies a strong individual-level
forecasting interpretation of the exploratory first check. This is a descriptive, previously exposed
12-person check, with neither an independent acquisition nor
an error-controlled prospective prediction claim. The source
models fitted across these inner folds still share participants,
and the 12-person dataset was previously exposed; source-choice
cross-fitting removes one form of selection reuse but is not a
separately validated prediction model. Five
incorrect directions and substantial error prevent using this
result as evidence of a reliable individual-level forecasting
rule. The preliminary and cross-fitted outcome receipts are the
successful workflows
\url{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38337523347}
and
\url{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38338129379},
respectively.
