BSPC research manuscript — draft highlights (author approval required)

- Class-centred EEG covariance scoring is not source-mean predictive likelihood.
- Its missing mean-shift term has three exact nonnegative components.
- Three EEG acquisitions show a strongly location-driven predictive-score gap.
- Source discrimination can help yet harm individual held-out participants.
- More precise source-only regularisation selection need not improve transfer.

These are working highlights for the evidence-gated manuscript, not publisher-submitted copy.
