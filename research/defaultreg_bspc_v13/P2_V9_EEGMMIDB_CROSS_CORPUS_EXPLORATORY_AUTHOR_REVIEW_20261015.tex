\subsection{Cross-corpus, development-frozen EEGMMIDB controlled provenance}
The original source-acquisition design from CHB--MIT differs from
PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery (EEGMMIDB).
To examine transfer beyond the initial EDF cohort, we evaluated
six digital EDF prefixes (S001--S006, R04). The EEG movement
imagery classes were deliberately never read or classified:
the endpoint is detection of numerical signal content reuse.
Each source EDF generated eight known controlled reuse
transforms and six designed unrelated or confounded targets.
There are six source records, not 84 independent acquisitions.

The tuned CAR comparator received the \emph{unchanged} thresholds
0.93 (training) and 0.90 (verification), previously selected
on the CHB--MIT development files chb01--02. The residual
witness retained its pre-existing training and verification
thresholds and did not refit on EEGMMIDB data.
Both methods rejected all 36 constructed negatives, while
the residual witness detected all 48/48 controlled
positives and frozen CAR detected 39/48. The raw
shared-clock matcher detected 48/48 positives but
falsely accepted 6/36 strong common-artifact negatives.
The between-method positive recall difference on the
controlled variants is $9/48=18.75$ percentage points.

All six EEGMMIDB source EDF files favored the residual
witness; their respective missed-CAR cases numbered
2, 1, 2, 1, 2 and 1. Treating each original EDF as
the paired inferential unit (rather than the dependent
transformed trials), the exploratory exact two-sided
sign test for six wins and zero losses gives
$p=2/2^6=0.03125$. Thus the test is nominally
significant against the \emph{particular, development-frozen
CAR comparator} under these controlled transfer conditions.
This endpoint and transformation suite evolved during
exploratory method development, so the nominal $p$ should
not be interpreted as a registered familywise-confirmatory
claim. The published algorithms for fingerprinting,
multi-device synchronization and nonlinear time alignment
have not been reproduced in this protocol.
Full GitHub Actions run:
\url{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38698819592};
the machine-readable source-file hashes and per-case
predictions are preserved in its uploaded artifact.

Both independent acquisition collections contain true
public EEG digital waveforms, but the putative reused
targets and severe common-mode challenges were generated
experimentally. Their labels do not establish naturally
duplicated patient recordings or independently adjudicated
clinical acquisition identity. Notably, the method's
superiority over a frozen CAR comparator does not prove
a universally lower false-match rate or a
state-of-the-art gain against fully informed published
synchronization and signal-fingerprint methods.
