\subsection{Certificate-first exact AUC event execution: research extension}
For a rational shrinkage-LDA pair margin, the common denominator
is strictly positive on the shrinkage interval. Before isolating
roots of its numerator polynomial $N_{ij}(\lambda)$, exact power
coefficient, quadratic extremum and Bernstein-sign tests can prove
the absence of interior zeros. No real-root solver is then called
for that pair. The other pairs use the \emph{identical exact
SymPy real-root backend} as the unpruned source-only incremental
event-sweep comparator. At each algebraic event, only incident
pair credits change; odd root multiplicity reverses the rank,
even multiplicity preserves the neighboring ranks, and the
event itself awards one-half for a tie. Endpoint and permanently
tied cases remain explicit. Under rational inputs, a strictly
positive definite covariance family and provably ordered
algebraic roots, this reproduces the full exact empirical
ROC--AUC path. Bernstein positivity and event sweeping are
classical; no new root-isolation theorem is asserted.

We fixed four rational source-path regimes with 12 seeds each
and 16 positive--negative score-polynomial pairs per seed.
All test polynomials are exactly representable by the
$d=3$ diagonal shrinkage-LDA family
$S=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,4)$, $\tau=7/3$ and
$d=(1,1,1)$ through inversion of the rational numerator
basis; they are not arbitrary unphysical sign functions.
The same incumbent incremental sweep and identical SymPy
root backend were used in both arms. The proposed
algorithm matched every exact AUC cell, isolated event
tie and domain endpoint in all 48 inputs.

Median runtime speed ratios were $9.72\times$ (stable ranks),
$3.87\times$ (sparse rank events), $2.08\times$ (mixed) and
$0.98\times$ (event dense). For the first three groups,
all 12/12 paired fixtures were faster; dense ranks gave
6/12 faster. Holm-adjusted one-sided paired Wilcoxon
$p=0.00098$ for each of the first three and
$p=0.455$ for dense events. These are small constructed
rational CPU timing comparisons, not real EEG
generalization, published root-isolator SOTA, or a
new numerical-theory priority claim. Performance on
external solvers, higher-dimensional EEG geometry,
full floating preprocessing uncertainty and unseen
acquisitions remains unestablished.
