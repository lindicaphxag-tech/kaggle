\paragraph{Second scoring implementation and source-only forecast.}
We implemented a pooled within-class covariance shrinkage LDA score
independently of the main spectral score, retaining the common EEG
covariance-to-tangent feature preprocessing. At each source fold,
the predictive Gaussian NLL uses class means fitted on the source
training participants; it does not re-centre the held person's class
labels. A source-person cross-fit forecasts the AUC difference
between source-macro and predictive-NLL selected shrinkage. The
score was evaluated for the nine BNCI2014\_001 held participants.
The executable process committed the eight-source selection for
held participant 9 before opening that signal, but the acquisition
and outcomes had previously been used in this research programme;
this remains a retrospective robustness check.

The source forecast identified the sign of the held AUC difference
for 7 of 9 participants. Mean absolute forecast error was
0.01538 AUC; the average observed source-macro minus correct
predictive-NLL AUC was +0.01261. The forecast missed the sign for
held participants 1 ($-0.00328$ observed versus +0.00204 forecast)
and 8 ($-0.01963$ versus +0.01323), and substantially underestimated
the positive difference for participant 9 (+0.06645 observed versus
+0.00694 forecast). These failures prevent claiming reliable
individual risk prediction or a new-experiment rule. The model
robustness test and nine scalar outcomes are in
\href{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38363352983}{the successful CI run}.
