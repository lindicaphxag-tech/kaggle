\subsection{Finite-sample validation-score identity and falsification tests}
\label{sec:p1-rational-witness}
For a \emph{fixed} positive-definite covariance $A$ and
held source-validation group $g$, write
$x_{gci}=\mu_{gc}+r_{gci}$ with
$\sum_i r_{gci}=0$ separately for each class $c$.
Let $\mu_{sc}$ be a training-source class mean that is
\emph{not} fitted using group $g$. The quadratic
part of the proper predictive Gaussian log score minus
the score centered at $\mu_{gc}$ is \emph{exactly}
\[
 \Delta_g={1\over2}\sum_c n_{gc}
 (\mu_{gc}-\mu_{sc})^\top A^{-1}
 (\mu_{gc}-\mu_{sc})\ \ge 0.
\]
Indeed, the cross terms in the squared residual
expansion vanish because the validation residuals
sum to zero within each group and class. The
log-determinant and Gaussian normalisation terms
cancel \emph{only because the covariance and
scoring sample are identical in both sides}.
This identity compares two \emph{scores}; it
does not predict which selector yields superior
unseen-participant AUC.

In the balanced two-class case with $n_{g0}=n_{g1}=k$,
let $h_c=\mu_{gc}-\mu_{sc}$,
$m=(h_0+h_1)/2$, $h=(h_1-h_0)/2$.
Then
\[
 \Delta_g=k\|m\|^2_{A^{-1}}
 +k\|h\|^2_{A^{-1}}.
\]
For a \emph{fixed} discriminant $w$, write the whitened
contrast $A^{-1/2}h$ as an orthogonal sum along
$A^{1/2}w$ and perpendicular to it. The resulting
three nonnegative terms are common location,
score-orthogonal class contrast and score-aligned
class contrast. The first two terms do not
change \emph{within-group} pairwise AUC when the
corresponding shifts are applied at fixed $w$ and
fixed preprocessing, whereas the third may change it.
The classification invariance is not an assertion of
full predictive-distribution invariance. In particular,
a subject-specific Riemannian reference fit would
invalidate this fixed-coordinate argument.

An independent exact-rational synthetic test
reproduces the finite-sample identity and confirms
both the invariant shifts and an aligned-shift
counterexample. This is a \emph{mathematical regression
test}, not a new EEG acquisition, not an independently
registered confirmation, and not an algorithmic
benefit experiment. The empirical three-source
score decomposition is separately reported with
its own retrospective evidence hierarchy.
