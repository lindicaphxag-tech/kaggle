\subsection{Prespecified multi-family exact-rational stress test}
\label{sec:p4-multifamily}
We further varied the spectral fixture without selecting an
interval from its observed outcome: three deterministic strictly
positive spectral families (linear, quadratic, and alternating),
five dimensions $p=2,3,6,9,12$, the same twelve deterministic
contrast patterns per dimension, and both eight and sixteen equal
rational subintervals. This produces 4,320 matched interval cases.
The numerator construction, coefficients, interval endpoints and
both methods use exact rational arithmetic. These are synthetic
classifier spectra, not an end-to-end certificate of the numerical
EEG preprocessing pipeline.

The Bernstein fallback raises the 
strict-sign count from 4,092 to 4,104 on this fixed
synthetic suite, a 0.278-percentage-point change in
coverage (12/4,320). It does not repair the 216 genuinely
sign-changing intervals and does not prove an asymptotic
speed advantage. In the earlier quadratic-spectrum 960-case
fixture, the advantage remains zero. This result narrows the
claim to a reproducible high-dimensional coarse-partition
condition; tests of rounding-safe floating implementations,
root isolation, runtime and more diverse polynomials remain
necessary before a numerical-algorithm journal submission.
