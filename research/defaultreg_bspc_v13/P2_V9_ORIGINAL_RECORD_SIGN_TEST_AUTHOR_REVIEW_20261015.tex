\subsection{A 12-record source-level audit of the tuned comparator}
We reused the frozen CAR training and verification thresholds
(0.93 and 0.90, respectively) selected on chb01--02,
and evaluated twelve additional CHB--MIT source EDF prefixes
(chb09--20), each under the same eight known controlled
positive transformations and six constructed negatives.
These additional source IDs were previously encountered
during corpus-level development, so they are \emph{not}
prospectively untouched clinical records.

Across the resulting 96 controlled positive cases,
the matched-subset residual verifier detected 96,
whereas tuned CAR followed by shared-clock matching
detected 91; both rejected all 72 constructed negatives.
The uncorrected raw matcher detected 96/96 but falsely
accepted 12/72 negative cases. The primary GitHub Actions
receipt was successful and preserves per-original
predictions at
\url{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38679288891}.

The five additional controlled detections attributed to
the residual verifier arise from only \emph{four} original
EDF sources: chb10 contributed two, and chb11, chb17
and chb19 contributed one each. The remaining eight
sources showed identical positives. Therefore an exact
paired sign test using \emph{original EDF files as the
units} has four positive and zero negative source-level
differences among four non-ties, yielding a
two-sided $p=2/2^4=0.125$ (preference-directed
one-sided $p=1/2^4=0.0625$). There is no familywise
significant or peer-validated state-of-the-art superiority
claim. Treating all 96 transformed positives as independent
would be statistical pseudoreplication.

A cross-corpus test on PhysioNet EEGMMIDB digital EDF
prefixes has been registered using the original frozen
CHB--MIT threshold without target retuning at
\url{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/workflows/p2-v9-eegmmidb-crosscorpus.yml}.
Its outcome must not be reported until the complete source
hashes, per-original case rows, and finalized workflow
receipt are verified. The method's legitimate endpoint is
\emph{numerical recording-content overlap}, not patient
identity or neural decoding accuracy.
