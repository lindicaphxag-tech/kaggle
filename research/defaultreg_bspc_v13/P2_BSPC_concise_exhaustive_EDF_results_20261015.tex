\subsection{Exhaustive public-EDF digital overlap census}
\label{sec:p2-main-census}
We re-downloaded all 50 publicly released Liu2024 preprocessed
EDF products and matched their full-file SHA-256 digests to a
version-pinned independent NEMAR distribution manifest. The
source-locked reader then compared all $\binom{50}{2}=1,225$
unordered cross-ID pairs at the same named channel and native
storage-record positions: 29 time-varying EEG channels by 320 EDF
records, or 9,280 potential block positions per pair. A positive
block match means equality of the as-stored integer digital
sample payload, \emph{not} calibrated physiological amplitude,
independent patient identity or an original acquisition session.

Only released IDs 4/5 shared all 29 complete EEG channel
streams and 9,280/9,280 aligned EEG blocks. IDs 47/49 shared
6,957/9,280 aligned EEG blocks without any whole time-varying
channel-stream identity; IDs 11/15 shared 171/9,280 blocks,
also without a complete-channel match. The remaining 1,222
ID pairs had zero matching blocks under this \emph{fixed and
aligned} comparison rule. These zero outcomes are descriptive
for the inspected release and cannot exclude transformed,
reordered, time-shifted or partly overlapping acquisitions.
The complete channel/block count table, source-file SHA
verification, alternative reader checks and source MAT
correspondence failures are retained in Supplementary Results
S2--S3. All analyses are retrospective, and no clinical
identity is inferred from digital sample equality.
