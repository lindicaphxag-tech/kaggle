\subsection{Locked-artifact common-policy null and oracle regret}
\label{sec:p3-two-original-run-null}
We independently re-read the \emph{original} real-EEG
GitHub Actions receipts from the five-policy BNCI2014\_004
held-ID-9 experiment (run 38341955398) and the
five-policy Weibo2014 fixed-source IDs-9/10 experiment
(run 38362787873). These contain three previously
exposed held records belonging to \emph{two distinct
acquisitions}, with different source models across
acquisitions; they must not be interpreted as three
independent acquisitions. Each comparison uses the
\emph{same five choices within its own source pool}:
source-macro scalar, continuous search, SCE,
anisotropic source risk and within-group centring.
The two original result artifacts and their original
byte digests are the audit inputs, rather than
manually transcribed three-decimal table entries.

The descriptive mean AUC differences relative to
source macro over the three held records are
0 (continuous), $-0.01121849$ (SCE),
$-0.00262667$ (anisotropic), and
$-0.00423547$ (group-centred).
The largest favourable \emph{per-record} change
by any nonbaseline method is
$+0.00040123$ AUC (anisotropic, BNCI ID 9);
on the two Weibo IDs every alternative method
either matches or underperforms source macro.
SCE has a worst observed paired deterioration of
$-0.021875$ AUC in Weibo ID 10.
Even a post-outcome \emph{per-person oracle}
that may select any of the five observed policies
would improve the original source-macro policy
by at most $0.00040123$ AUC on any one of
these \emph{specific} records. The observed
retrospective constant-policy mean is maximised
by source macro or the identical continuous path.

This is a weak \emph{negative control} on a small
previously examined target selection, not an
equivalence test, proof of no possible useful
source-only rule, or a fair estimate of a new
population-level effect. Acquisition-level $n=2$
is inadequate for a confirmatory meta-inference.
The exercise isolates the \emph{additional decision
benefit} that a novel selector would need to
demonstrate on new uninspected acquisitions,
rather than mistaking nontrivial policy
complexity for predictive utility.
