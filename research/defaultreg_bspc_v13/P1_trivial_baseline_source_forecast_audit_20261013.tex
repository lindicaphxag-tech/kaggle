\subsection{A necessary trivial-baseline falsification of source-only direction forecasts}
\label{sec:p1-forecast-null}
The second shrinkage-LDA source forecast was re-audited directly from the
previously published nine-participant scalar receipt, using the original
participant as the unit of calculation. Its source-only predictions of
source-macro minus proper-predictive AUC were positive in
\emph{all nine} participants. The observed signs were positive for
seven participants and negative for two. Accordingly its $7/9$
directional accuracy is \emph{exactly} the accuracy of the trivial
always-positive predictor and supplies no evidence of an improved sign
classifier.

The mean absolute deviation from the observed AUC margin is
$0.015381$ with the source predictor and $0.017699$ with the
zero-margin reference forecast; the descriptive improvement is
$0.002318$ AUC. A two-sided exact paired sign-flip enumeration over
the nine absolute-error differences returns $p=0.3203125$.
This enumeration is explicitly \emph{exploratory}: the predictor
and comparator outcomes were previously exposed, outer source folds
overlap, and the exchangeable-sign premise of confirmatory paired
randomisation inference is not independently established.
Neither an improved sign-forecast method nor a generalisable
positive margin prediction follows from these results.
The independent acquisition-level prospective risk-prediction
gate remains open.
