\paragraph{Retrospective replication of four operators in a second acquisition.}
To test whether the five-policy comparison for BNCI2014\_004 was
specific to that acquisition, we applied the identical source-macro,
continuous, source-contrast envelope (SCE), anisotropic and
group-centred operators to Weibo2014. We froze choices using source
participants 1--8 before reading participants 9 and 10 in the
executed process, and evaluated both using the \emph{same} frozen
source model. Each held participant had 160 trials, 80 in each class.
These two participants belong to one acquisition, and both target
outcomes had been exposed in earlier work in this project; this is
\emph{retrospective second-acquisition sensitivity}, not an
independently blinded prospective test.
