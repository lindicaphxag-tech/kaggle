\paragraph{Strictly nested source-person cross-fit: correcting indirect re-entry.}
\label{sec:p1-strict-nested-source-audit}
An audit of the independent LDA source-only
forecast implementation identified a leakage
path \emph{within the source-person forecast}:
the earlier leave-one-source-person-out prediction
excluded the held person's \emph{validation score}
when choosing each estimator, but validation rows
from other source people could have been produced
by cross-validation models trained using this
held person's data. This violates the interpretation
of the source forecast as a strictly outer-person
out-of-fold prediction, though it does \emph{not}
imply that AlexMI's reserved target participants
7 and 8 entered either source fit.

We corrected this with a full \emph{nested}
six-person source experiment. For each outer
source person $h\in\{1,\ldots,6\}$, we first
remove all data from $h$, recompute source
tangent reference, perform the inner
five-participant source-person cross-validation
on the unchanged 27-point shrinkage grid, choose
the macro-AUC and proper predictive-NLL parameters,
refit both classifiers on the five remaining
people, and evaluate $h$ exactly once.
Consequently, no model or geometry involved
in the choice for $h$ has seen $h$.
The six outer-person AUC differences
(macro minus proper) were
$\{+.0925,+.1850,-.0400,+.0200,-.0500,+.1500\}$,
with mean $+.05958$ AUC; the earlier
source-fold estimator reported $+.05417$
but lacked this strict outer-person exclusion.

The frozen choices fit on all six people
remain $\lambda_{\rm macro}=1.0$ and
$\lambda_{\rm proper}=.20$.
When used as a single positive forecast for
the \emph{previously inspected} AlexMI held
people 7 and 8, the strict source-only
forecast retains the correct positive
directions, while their actual macro-minus-proper
margins remain $+.0050$ and $+.1875$.
The trivial always-positive forecast makes
the same two directional predictions.
No individual risk prediction advantage
over that null, external preregistration or
new target-subject generalisation is inferred.
The source-person strict chronology, all
inner folds and the complete numerical
receipt were checked in the successful
\href{https://github.com/lindicaphxag-tech/kaggle/actions/runs/38539884099}
{GitHub Actions run 38539884099}.
This correction should precede any
future source-forecast accuracy comparison;
the earlier heuristic cross-fit numbers
must not be described as independent
outer-person predictions.
